# 🪷 Velora

> **Think. Design. Create.**

**Velora** is an AI-powered career assistant designed to help students and job seekers build stronger resumes, improve their career readiness, identify skill gaps, prepare for interviews, and receive personalized career guidance through a unified platform.

Built with a focus on **AI, user-centered design, and modern frontend development**, Velora brings essential career tools together in one clean and intuitive workspace.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📄 AI Resume Analyzer

* Analyze resumes using ATS-focused criteria
* Generate an ATS compatibility score
* Identify missing sections and important resume elements
* Provide actionable suggestions for improvement

### ✨ AI Resume Generator
- Generate personalized resumes for different career roles
- Create role-specific resumes based on the user's skills, experience, and career goals
- Tailor resume content and sections to suit different professional profiles
- Generate multiple resume versions for different job applications

### ✍️ AI Resume Improvement

* Improve resume content using AI
* Rewrite weak or unclear resume descriptions
* Enhance professional language

* Generate professional cover letters

* Get personalized career guidance
* Explore possible career directions
* Receive AI-powered recommendations

### 🗺️ Career Roadmap

* Build a structured career development path
* Identify skills and areas to learn
* Track progress toward career goals

### 📊 Progress Tracking

* Monitor career preparation progress
* Track improvements across different career activities

Support for exporting career documents in multiple formats:

* PDF

## 💡 The Idea Behind Velora

### How Velora Started

The idea for **Velora** started when I noticed that many websites charge money for features like full resume analysis, ATS checking, resume building, and professional templates.

As students, we can create resumes using **Canva or MS Word**, but there is always uncertainty about whether the resume will actually pass an ATS or be suitable for a particular job. Sometimes, a resume may look good to us but still have a 50/50 chance of getting through an ATS.

I wanted to change that.

Instead of making students pay for every career-related feature, I wanted to create a platform where essential career tools are **free and accessible to students** — from ATS checking and detailed resume analysis to professional resume templates and resume creation.

That idea became **Velora**.

My vision is to build **one place for everything a student needs for their career journey** — creating and improving resumes, checking ATS compatibility, matching resumes with job descriptions, identifying skill gaps, preparing for interviews, generating cover letters, exploring career paths, and getting AI-powered career guidance.

Velora is built around a simple thought:

**Why should students pay for multiple tools when they can have everything they need in one place?**

So, Velora isn't just a resume builder. It is my attempt to create a **complete, accessible career companion for students**.
